This patch is an upgraded/complete version of a previous Partner Only patch that I made. This patch is entirely beatable from
begining to end without any need for uninstalling, unlike the first version. I made a handful of changes to progression and 
behaviors to prevent softlocks/entering battles when Mario is alone. This mod should be entirely Practice Codes compatible. 

Key Changes:
Mario cannot attack, be targeted, take other actions, take damage, be first struck, make first strikes, or do basically anything
in battle. His turn is skipped in its entirety. For convenience I removed field hazard damage as well. Mario cannot be targeted
by partner moves or items either.
Partners are the only ones who can take damage and attack. All first strikes are aimed at the partner.
Mario is no longer the one who decides if game overs happen in battle, if your partner is defeated a game over will happen.
Battle positions for Mario and Partner have been swapped, Partner will always be in front and Mario will be in the back.
The ability to switch positions has been disabled.
Goombella can tattle as many times in a turn as she wants but you do not gain star power for it anymore.
In the first Crump fight you will be joined by Goombella who can actually attack.
In the Doopliss rematch Vivian will join you on the first turn.
Once Doopliss steals your body and you attempt to exit his room you are warped straight to twilight town. This was required
to prevent Mario alone battles that are unwinnable since you cant attack. Sequences are updated properly so think of it like
you get to skip some backtracking. Also the twilight town gate locks again until you get Vivian to prevent going back to Doopliss
without Vivian.
Any instances of "Use A Special Move" or "Dont Let Your Partner Attack" in the Glitz Pit have been removed. 
The Poison Cake event has also been removed.
I included the unused Excess Express patch that I made as well, so theres an extra Power Punch in there for those who want it!
